## Capture and review

Use the ribbon buttons or the command palette (Ctrl+P):
- **QuickAdd: Capture Thought** or **Capture URL**
- **QuickAdd: Capture Task** opens TaskNotes' task form
- **QuickAdd: New Project**
- **QuickAdd: Open Weekly Review** creates or opens this week's review
- **QuickAdd: Open Monthly Review** creates or opens this month's review
